1. Team Introduction
   – Team/company name, member names and photos.
2. Problem & Proposed Idea
   – What problem do you want to solve? Who may have this problem?
   – What is your proposed product/service? How could it help solve the problem?
3. Why This Idea?
   – Why did your team choose this idea?
   – What does your team know about this field?
   – What is new, creative or technology-related about the idea?
4. Market Overview
   – Who are your initial target customers/users?
   – Give a brief overview of the market/industry, relevant trends or facts, and existingproducts/services.
5. How Could It Work?
   – Who are the main stakeholders?
   – What technology/resources are needed? Can your team develop the core product/service?– What partners or third parties may be needed?
6. Business Potential
   – Who might pay and what could they pay for?
   – What could be the main revenue source(s)?
   – Could the idea grow or scale in the future?
7. From Idea to Company
   – Mission and Vision.
   – Simple organization chart.
   – Roles and main responsibilities of each member.
   – Relevant skills/knowledge of the team.
8. Next Steps
   – What will your team do after CP1?
   – What do you need to learn or research next?

• Slides must be in English, professional and formal.
• Keep the presentation short and clear (10–15 slides).
